Why this excuse: it is rooted in the robot vacuum's own world — its map —
and the joke lands on the first read, with the sting in the last words.

`/output/entry.txt` and asserts the gate itself: 1–140 characters, not only
white space. It is deterministic — the same bytes on every run.
